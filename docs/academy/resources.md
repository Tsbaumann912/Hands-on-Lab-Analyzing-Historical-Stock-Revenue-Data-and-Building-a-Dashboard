## Video-oriented starting points

Search these titles on the linked channels (official uploads change IDs):

1. **CME Group YouTube** — “Introduction to Futures”, “How Futures Work”,
   product playlists (Equity Index, Energy, FX futures).
2. **CQG / CME blog** — [Introduction to Futures Video Course](https://news.cqg.com/blogs/2022/02/introduction-futures-video-course)
3. **Babypips lessons** — each School of Pipsology lesson page embeds or links
   lesson media; complete Preschool → Graduation path.
4. **Federal Reserve / central bank explainers** — search YouTube for
   “FOMC explained” on reputable news or Fed channels when studying S20–S21.
