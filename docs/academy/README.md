# QuantTerminal Academy — Futures & Forex Profit Strategies

Educational curriculum covering the major **families of strategies** used to generate
returns in futures and foreign-exchange (FX) markets. This is a taxonomy of
profit engines — not a promise that any single setup is profitable, and not
investment advice.

## How to use this course

1. Start with **Module 00** (market foundations) even if you already trade.
2. Study one strategy family at a time; each lesson names the edge hypothesis,
   failure modes, and risk controls.
3. Watch the linked videos, then open QuantTerminal’s **Strategies** lab to
   backtest related systematic variants (trend, momentum, mean reversion).
4. Finish with **Module 09** (multi-strategy portfolios) — diversification across
   uncorrelated edges matters more than hunting a “holy grail” setup.

## Disclaimers

- **Educational only.** Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any
  instrument.
- **Leverage kills.** Futures and FX are margined products; account wipeouts are
  common without strict risk limits.
- **Past pattern ≠ future edge.** Many published strategies decay after
  overcrowding; always validate out-of-sample.
- Video and article links are third-party educational materials; QuantTerminal
  does not control their content or availability.
